About Our Learning Hub

This interactive portal is developed by Group 3 to showcase key concepts from Empowerment Technologies, focusing on web structures, ICT's role in modern society, and essential online safety guidelines.

Core Learning Pillars

🌐

Types of Websites

Exploring static vs. dynamic web pages, web development fundamentals, and structural layouts that drive interactive online experiences.

🏛️

ICT Sectors & Citizenship

Understanding the major sectors of Information & Communications Technology and practicing responsible digital citizenship online.

🛡️

Dangers of the Internet

Recognizing online threats—from phishing scams to cyberbullying—and mastering practical strategies for personal internet safety.

Key Topics Covered

Web Development ICT Sectors Digital Citizenship Online Safety Phishing Prevention Anti-Cyberbullying

Our Objective

To equip 21st-century learners with actionable ICT skills to navigate web structures, engage responsibly as digital citizens, and safeguard themselves against modern internet threats.
