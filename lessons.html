Topic 1: 📒 Types of Websites

▼

Topic Summary

A website is a collection of publicly accessible, interlinked web pages that share a single domain name. Websites generally fall into two main categories: Static Websites (which display the same fixed content to every visitor) and Dynamic Websites (which change content in real-time based on user interaction, databases, or user accounts). Common types of websites include blogs, e-commerce platforms, portfolio sites, educational portals, and social media networks—each serving unique communication and functional purposes in modern ICT.

Topic 2: 💼 ICT Sectors & Digital Citizenship

▼

Topic Summary

Information and Communications Technology (ICT) encompasses key industrial sectors including Telecommunications, Software Engineering, Web Development, and IT Support. Beyond hardware and software, navigating these sectors requires practicing responsible Digital Citizenship—understanding netiquette, respecting intellectual property, protecting personal data privacy, and practicing safe, ethical digital behavior across online platforms.

Topic 3: 🛡️ Dangers of the Internet & Online Safety

▼

Topic Summary

While the internet provides powerful tools for communication and learning, it also presents significant risks such as cyberbullying, malware infections (viruses, trojans, ransomware), phishing scams, and privacy breaches. Practicing digital citizenship, maintaining strong password security, verifying online sources, and fostering respectful online interactions are essential to navigating the digital world safely.
